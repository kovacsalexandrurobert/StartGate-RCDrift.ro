Start Light

Wiring schematic

Pico W → 220Ω → SSR → race lamps. Four channels, active-low, optional OLED on I2C.

View firmware on GitHub Pin map

GPIO map

One SSR per color. Wire extra lamp trees in parallel on the same SSR output. Active-low: GPIO low turns the light on.

Color GPIO Pico pin SSR Connects to
Red GP2 4 SSR1 All red lamps (parallel)
Yellow 1 GP3 5 SSR2 All first yellow lamps
Yellow 2 GP4 6 SSR3 All second yellow lamps
Green GP5 7 SSR4 All green lamps
OLED SDA GP6 9 — SSD1306 SDA (optional)
OLED SCL GP7 10 — SSD1306 SCL (optional)

Power & notes

Keep Pico, 5V supply, and LED supply grounds common. Lamp rails are 5–12 V through the SSRs; control side is 3.3 V from the Pico.

  • Pico power: 5 V on VBUS (pin 40), GND on pin 38 — USB or a 5 V adapter.
  • SSR input: Logic-level, 3.3 V compatible, ~10 mA through a 220–330 Ω resistor per channel.
  • OLED: SSD1306 128×64 I2C at 0x3C on GP6/GP7; VCC from Pico 3V3. Firmware does not probe 0x3D.
  • Build tip: Test one color before paralleling multiple lamp sets on an SSR.
